Step-by-Step Guide to Placing Free Nationwide Calls

1 Step 1: Accessing the Calling Interface

  • Approach any active standard kiosk and tap the center of the built-in Android tablet screen.
  • Select the prominent Phone Icon or "Make a Call" shortcut button from the main application dashboard layout.

2 Step 2: Keying the Destination Number

  • An on-screen digital dial pad will instantly render. Key in the 10-digit phone number (including area code) for any location inside the United States.

  • Tap the green Call Button on the screen to activate the line. The kiosk uses a built-in microphone and speakers to handle audio.

  • Tap the red End Call toggle on the touchscreen once your session concludes to clear your history.
