Essential VPN Settings for Sidewalk Commuters

The correct deployment: Simply downloading a security utility is not enough to guarantee data insulation near high-traffic public kiosks. You must manually optimize your application configurations to prevent accidental clear-text leaks.

  • Activate Auto-Connect: Enable rules that forcefully launch the VPN tunnel the exact second your device touches an open wireless SSID.
  • Enforce WireGuard Protocol: Switch your connection framework from older OpenVPN layers to WireGuard to maintain maximum data speeds on gigabit fiber.
  • Lock the Kill Switch: Ensure the application cuts all outbound device internet traffic immediately if the VPN connection drops unexpectedly.
