The visual shift: The most obvious difference between the two communication models is their size and scale on the sidewalk canvas.

Technical Connectivity and Range Benchmarks

Under the hood: The internal components inside these structures alter how data packets move through the air to reach your phone antenna.

  • Wi-Fi Spectrum: Both models deliver free gigabit public Wi-Fi via localized fiber-optic links connected to the city's under-street framework.
  • Cellular Integration: Standard kiosks provide no cellular transmission. Link5G poles house actual multi-operator cellular antennas inside their upper bays.
  • Data Frequencies: Link5G integrates lightning-fast millimeter wave (mmWave) and mid-band radio frequencies to bypass dense urban skyscraper interference.
  • Coverage Radius: Standard hotspots carry a dependable 100-to-150-foot reach, while the taller Link5G towers securely broadcast signals up to 250 feet away.
