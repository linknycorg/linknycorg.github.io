Location Profile & Surrounding Area Context

Positioned strategically along the active corridor at 2-29 BEACH 20TH STREET in Queens's lively Elmhurst sector, this Link5G Smart Pole represents a crucial piece of civic hardware. Featuring a high-contrast interactive layout and next-generation signal transceivers, the terminal extends secure, ultra-high-speed public web links. Beyond the internet, the structure provides high-amperage charging connections and an analog-accessible 911 helpline.

Situated in a vibrant sector of the street, this kiosk is accompanied by a colorful selection of neighborhood businesses. With Beach Boutique & Gifts located practically on top of the node and The Beach Way Medical Plaza just next door, the terminal is a dynamic charging point. Directly facing the terminal, the opposite avenue frontage hosts a historic brownstone residential block, providing a classic borough backdrop.
