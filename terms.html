Description of Service & High Utility Directory

WiFiNYC.app operates as a standalone, mobile-first one-page web application that serves as an independent public utility directory. The App utilizes official Government API endpoints (NYC Open Data) to display the geographic distribution of free Wi-Fi kiosks and 5G smart poles across New York City. We provide this mapping tool and related infrastructure information purely for community utility and educational purposes.
