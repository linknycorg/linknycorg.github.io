Defeating Juice Jacking: Hardware Security Protocols

The data protection: A common concern among tech-savvy commuters is "juice jacking"—a cyber attack where modified public USB ports steal local phone data or upload tracking malware during a charging session.

  • Power-Only Engineering The integrated LinkNYC USB pins are hardwired exclusively for electricity delivery, meaning the data transfer pins are physically absent from the hardware circuit.
  • Voltage Regulation Shields Internal systems monitor power stability, protecting your delicate lithium-ion smartphone battery from unexpected grid power surges.
  • Pro Tip for Absolute Safety Use a simple, data-blocking USB condom or a standard wall adapter attached to a nearby store outlet if you require absolute physical data isolation.
