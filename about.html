Our Mission: Empowering Urban Connectivity

WiFiNYC.app is a standalone, mobile-first web directory dedicated to mapping and monitoring the digital evolution of New York City. Our mission is simple: to provide NYC residents, commuters, and tourists with an instant, high-utility visual tool to locate free public Wi-Fi kiosks, high-speed Link5G smart poles, and charging stations across all five boroughs.

02

Why It Matters: Smart Brevity for Commuters

The Big Picture

New York City is a fast-paced metropolis where staying connected is essential for work, transit, and safety. However, mobile data caps, underground signal drops, and battery emergencies can disrupt your daily routine.

The Solution

Our independent platform bridges the gap by transforming raw municipal infrastructure data into a clean, interactive, and mobile-optimized locator app. We help you find free gigabit internet access within walking distance, wherever you are in the city.

03

Tech Stack & Native Infrastructure

We believe in fast loading times and absolute transparency. This application does not use heavy external tracking frameworks or illegal content frames.

Socrata API

Real-Time Data

Our map infrastructure communicates directly with the official NYC Open Data live catalog via native API authentication. This ensures that the thousands of active nodes displayed on your screen are accurate and updated.

optimized

High Performance

By leveraging vanilla JavaScript and optimized marker clustering algorithms, this app runs smoothly on any mobile browser, ensuring commuters can load the map in less than a second while walking down Manhattan or Brooklyn.

offline-first

PWA Integration

Our built-in Progressive Web App (PWA) technology allows you to save this tool directly to your device with 0 MB of hardware storage, keeping the map functional even when you encounter transit dead-zones.
