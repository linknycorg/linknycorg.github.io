Get in Touch with the WiFiNYC.app Team

The Big Picture

WiFiNYC.app is a privately-owned, independent public utility directory. We are dedicated to providing NYC residents and tourists with accurate mapping of free public Wi-Fi kiosks and Link5G smart poles.

Why It Matters

To maintain the highest quality database in Silicon Alley, we rely on real-time feedback from our community. Whether you want to report a broken kiosk marker, suggest a feature, or explore business partnerships, we are here to listen.

01

How We Can Help You

Technical Support

If the dynamic map is lagging on your mobile phone, or if the borough quick-filters are not updating properly on your device, let us know your phone model and browser type so our engineering team can fix it.

Kiosk Data Updates

Notice a new Link5G smart pole installed in your Brooklyn or Queens neighborhood that is not showing up on our map? Send us the cross-streets or zip code, and we will trigger an manual API sync with the NYC Open Data catalog.

Advertising & B2B

If you are a local NYC business owner (such as a coffee shop, local deli, or restaurant) located near a high-traffic LinkNYC kiosk and want to lease a premium ad placement on our directory to drive foot traffic, our marketing team can assist you with custom CPM packages.

02

Independent Public Utility Notice (Reminder)

Please Note: WiFiNYC.app is an independent platform and does NOT operate the physical Wi-Fi kiosks, charging ports, or tablet screens on the streets of New York. If you are experiencing a hardware problem, lost an item near a kiosk, or need to make an emergency call from a physical station, you must contact the official government CityBridge management directly.
